How the Scrabble Solver Works

Our official Scrabble Word Finder checks your rack letters against standard tournament dictionary lists (TWL06, ENABLE1, and SOWPODS). Words are instantly rendered with authentic Scrabble tile values and sorted by highest total points first.

Pro Tips for High Scrabble Scores

  • Hooking: Place letters perpendicular to existing words on the board to form multiple words in a single turn.
  • Bingos: Play all 7 tiles on your rack to score a massive 50-point bonus.
  • Tile Control: Keep an equal mix of vowels and consonants on your rack for your next turn.
