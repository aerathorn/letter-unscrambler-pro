How to Use Letter Unscrambler Pro

Letter Unscrambler Pro is a free online tool that helps word puzzle enthusiasts solve scrambled words, anagrams, and tile games like Scrabble, Words with Friends, and Wordle.

  1. Type your scrambled letters into the search bar above.
  2. (Optional) Choose a specific word length filter if you are looking for an exact match.
  3. Click Unscramble to generate all possible word combinations sorted by length.

Tips for High Scrabble Scores

When playing Scrabble or Words with Friends, look for opportunities to play 7-letter words to earn a 50-point bonus (known as a "Bingo"). Focusing on high-value letters like Q, Z, X, and J can also drastically increase your score!
