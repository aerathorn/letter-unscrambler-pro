How to Use Letter Unscrambler Pro

Letter Unscrambler Pro is a free online tool that helps word puzzle enthusiasts solve scrambled words, anagrams, and tile games like Scrabble, Words with Friends, and Wordle.

  1. Type your scrambled letters into the search bar.
  2. Select your preferred scoring system (Scrabble or Words With Friends).
  3. (Optional) Use advanced filters like Starts With, Ends With, or Contains.
  4. Click Unscramble to generate all possible word combinations sorted by highest point score first.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How does the letter unscrambler work?

Simply enter your scrambled rack letters into the input box and click Unscramble. The tool compares your letters against our official English word dictionary to generate all possible valid words instantly.

What is the difference between Scrabble and Words With Friends scoring?

While most tile point values are identical, a few letters have different scores. For example, 'J' is worth 8 points in Scrabble but 10 points in Words With Friends. You can switch between scoring systems using our dropdown menu.

Is this letter unscrambler tool free?

Yes, Letter Unscrambler Pro is 100% free to use on mobile, tablet, and desktop with no sign-up or registration required.
