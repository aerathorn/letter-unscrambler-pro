Frequently Asked Questions

Find instant answers to common questions about using Letter Unscrambler Pro, scoring rules, and search filters.

1. How does the letter unscrambler work?

Simply enter your scrambled rack letters into the main search box and click Unscramble. The tool compares your letters against our official English word dictionary to generate all possible valid words instantly.

2. What is the difference between Scrabble and Words With Friends scoring?

While most tile point values are identical, a few letters have different scores. For example, 'J' is worth 8 points in Scrabble but 10 points in Words With Friends. You can switch between scoring systems using our dropdown menu.

3. How do I use the Starts With, Ends With, and Contains filters?

Click on the filter chips underneath the search box to expand fields. Enter the specific prefix, suffix, or letters you want to match. Our Smart Merge algorithm will automatically incorporate those constraint letters into your search.

4. Which dictionary does Letter Unscrambler Pro use?

Our tool uses the standard tournament English dictionary, which combines official word lists from ENABLE1, SOWPODS, and TWL06 to ensure complete coverage for word game players.

5. Are words sorted by highest point score?

Yes! Words are grouped by letter length (e.g., 7-letter, 6-letter) and automatically sorted from highest point value to lowest point value so you can easily find your winning move.

6. Is Letter Unscrambler Pro free?

Yes, Letter Unscrambler Pro is 100% free to use on mobile, tablet, and desktop with no sign-up or registration required.
